## General Direction

Use a calm nonprofit health publication style: realistic, warm, trustworthy, and practical. Avoid anything that feels like a hospital advertisement, fitness influencer post, pharmaceutical ad, or generic stock photo.

Preferred visual palette:

- Deep green: `#005030`
- Coral orange: `#f07239`
- Navy: `#0d1338`
- Soft mint: `#e8f7f0`
- Warm white: `#fff8f2`

Export guidance:

- Hero image: `1600 x 900`, 16:9 landscape, WebP.
- Standard article images: `1400 x 933`, 3:2 landscape, WebP.
- Infographics: `1200 x 1200`, square, WebP or PNG if text is sharper.
- Keep file sizes ideally under `350 KB` after compression.
- Avoid visible brand logos, fake product labels, impossible medical devices, extra fingers, distorted hands, and unreadable micro-text.
- Include diverse, realistic adults without making diabetes visually stereotyped or scary.
- If text appears inside an image, keep it minimal, large, high-contrast, and proofread carefully.
