# Accessibility Review Notes

- Text in the PDF is selectable.
- Major sections are represented with PDF bookmarks.
- Links are embedded and descriptive.
- Meaningful image alt text is documented in `Research/image-license-manifest.csv`.
- Color is paired with labels, not used alone for essential meaning.
- Full PDF/UA tagging should be completed in Acrobat, LibreOffice, or a dedicated remediation tool before clinical/community distribution.
