# Free Health Resources

Practical guidance should be easy to understand and easy to access. These free Mindful Diabetes guides turn complicated health topics into clear explanations, everyday examples, and printable tools.

These resources are for general education. They do not replace medical care, personalized nutrition advice, medication guidance, or urgent medical attention.

## The Mindful Plate

A friendly guide to building blood sugar-conscious meals with vegetables, protein, carbohydrates, drinks, portions, and flexible real-life examples.

Who this is for: For adults with type 2 diabetes, prediabetes, family history, caregivers, or anyone who wants clearer meal guidance.

Topics: Nutrition, Blood sugar, Meal planning

Suggested button: Download the Free Guide

## Fats Without Fear

A clear, nonjudgmental guide to saturated, unsaturated, and trans fats, with practical swaps for everyday meals.

Who this is for: For readers confused by fat advice who want heart-conscious choices without food fear.

Topics: Dietary fats, Heart health, Food swaps

Suggested button: Download the Free Guide

## The Grocery Store Survival Guide

A practical shopping guide for labels, budget choices, pantry foods, drinks, snacks, and fast meal formulas.

Who this is for: For anyone who wants to shop with less overwhelm and more confidence.

Topics: Grocery shopping, Food labels, Budget meals

Suggested button: Download the Free Guide

## The 7-Day Prevention Reset

A gentle seven-day habit guide for meals, drinks, movement, sleep, tracking, and next-week planning.

Who this is for: For readers who want a realistic one-week starting point without a crash diet or cleanse.

Topics: Habits, Prevention, Trackers

Suggested button: Download the Free Guide

## Blood Sugar & Brain Health

A careful guide to glucose, insulin resistance, blood vessels, dementia risk, and everyday habits that may support brain health.

Who this is for: For adults and caregivers who want a calm explanation of diabetes and long-term cognitive health.

Topics: Brain health, Diabetes, Prevention

Suggested button: Download the Free Guide
