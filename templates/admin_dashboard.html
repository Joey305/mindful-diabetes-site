  • Sessions are anonymous browser sessions, not identified people.
  • Donation clicks and PayPal opens are not confirmed donations.
  • A source page is the page where the visitor clicked.
  • Click-through rate is clicks divided by page views in the selected period.
  • Temporary storage may reset after a restart or deployment.
