Volunteer with Mindful Diabetes

Help grow prevention education, one useful contribution at a time.

Mindful Diabetes is building education, research awareness, wellness tools, and community support around Type II and Type III diabetes prevention. Volunteers can help us reach more people, make our tools better, and turn good ideas into work that actually moves.

Raise your hand Explore opportunities
8 ways to help

Writing, outreach, research support, design, social media, tool testing, fundraising, and partnerships.

Why volunteers matter

A small nonprofit grows through thoughtful hands

We do not need every volunteer to do everything. We need people who can bring one useful skill, one steady habit, or one small pocket of time to the mission. Whether you are comfortable writing, researching, organizing, designing, testing tools, or connecting us with new audiences, there is a meaningful place to plug in.

Opportunities

Choose a lane that fits your strengths

01

Guide writing and editing

Help turn diabetes, brain health, nutrition, and prevention topics into clear articles people can actually understand and use.

02

Research summaries

Support our research page and guide by helping summarize studies, organize citations, and connect science back to daily prevention habits.

03

Social media storytelling

Create short posts, captions, carousels, and video ideas that help Mindful Diabetes reach families, caregivers, students, and wellness-minded communities.

04

Community outreach

Help identify schools, clinics, running clubs, local organizations, and wellness groups that may want to share or support our prevention resources.

05

Tool testing and feedback

Try JEIR, Memovela, and the Mindful Eating Game, then share honest feedback on clarity, usability, mobile experience, and what would make them more helpful.

06

Design and media support

Help polish graphics, article images, video thumbnails, educational one-pagers, and visual assets that make the mission feel more trustworthy and alive.

07

Fundraising and events

Support small campaigns, book fundraiser outreach, donor updates, and community events that help fund education and prevention work.

08

Partnership scouting

Help us find aligned researchers, health creators, sponsors, community leaders, and organizations that can amplify the work responsibly.

How it starts

Start simple, then build momentum

Volunteering should feel clear and manageable. We can begin with one small task, learn what fits, and grow from there.

Step 1

Tell us your lane

Send a short note with what you are interested in, what skills you enjoy using, and how much time you realistically have.

Step 2

Pick a first task

We will match you with a focused first contribution, such as reviewing a tool, drafting a caption, or summarizing one topic.

Step 3

Keep what works

If it feels useful and sustainable, we can turn that first contribution into a recurring role or a larger project.

Get involved

Ready to help Mindful Diabetes grow?

Send a quick email with your name, the volunteer lane that interests you, and one sentence about why the mission speaks to you.
