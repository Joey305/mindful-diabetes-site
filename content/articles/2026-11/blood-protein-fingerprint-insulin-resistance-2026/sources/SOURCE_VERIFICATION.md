### Source-data landing point

The Nature Metabolism paper has linked Statistical Source Data for Figs. 2–7, and supplementary tables 1–9. Follow the primary paper's "Source data" and "Supplementary information" links; do not fabricate measurements or plot shape from AI illustration.

## What these sources do NOT show

- The November study did not establish an FDA-approved or clinically implemented 13-protein diagnostic assay.
- The 488 associated proteins were not shown to cause insulin resistance.
- A tissue-of-origin annotation from the Human Protein Atlas is not direct evidence of secretion or release from that organ in any specific study participant.
- The study did not test whether reporting protein scores improves patient outcomes, how changing treatment based on scores affects health, or whether it predicts dementia.
- The exercise cohort results cannot be generalized confidently across all sexes, ancestries, ages, medications and forms of exercise.
