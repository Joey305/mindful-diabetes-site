# Mindful Diabetes scientific figure corrections

Primary source: Kjærgaard J, Kurgan N, Gräf JF, et al. *Nature Metabolism*, 16 November 2026. https://doi.org/10.1038/s42255-026-01662-1

The selected illustrated series is retained. The supplied MDI logo is inserted directly. No Figure N banners.

## Figure 1

Skeletal muscle now correctly identified as a major site of insulin-stimulated glucose uptake. The incorrect "myokine of glucose uptake" text is removed. Anatomical art is conceptual.

## Figure 2

"My value" corrected to "M value"; ~90 mg/dL is explicitly called an illustrative clamp target, not the specific study set point.

## Figure 3

Discovery-cohort garbled label replaced with "488 associations". Coverage (>8,000 across platforms) is distinguished from the 488/62 discovery statistical associations; misleading arrows no longer imply a universal sample filter.

## Figure 4

Overlay explicitly identifies tissue associations as HPA-based inference and not directly measured secretion or organ-to-organ protein traffic.

## Figure 5

Replaced female athlete and invented error-bar chart with a male HIIT participant and an explicit nonquantitative direction-of-change schematic. Reported results: 43 men, eight weeks of HIIT, ~30–40% increase in M value, 22 significantly changed plasma proteins.

## Figure 6

Added independent validation n=43 label; R² values (0.73 combined vs 0.49 clinical alone) describe independent validation of clamp-derived insulin sensitivity estimation, not a disease risk score.

## Figure 7

Clarified that C-index values 0.86/0.88/0.74 quantify UK Biobank incident type 2 diabetes discrimination; they are not personal risk probabilities. HbA1c alone was slightly higher in this comparison.

## Scientific status

The graphics are educational adaptations, not original journal figures or raw participant scans. Fig. 5 shows directional schematic, **not experimental effect sizes**. The separate 13-protein score should not be called a clinically available diagnostic test.
