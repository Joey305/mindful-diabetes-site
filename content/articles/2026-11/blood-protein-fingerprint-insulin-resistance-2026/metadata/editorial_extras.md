## Short research social teaser

Researchers measured insulin sensitivity in 161 people, profiled thousands of circulating proteins, and developed a model using 13 proteins plus routine clinical data. It's early research, not a medical test you can order today. The bigger question: what can blood proteins tell us about metabolism?
