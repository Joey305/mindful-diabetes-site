# Editorial claim boundary / fact-check checklist

| Risk | Safe wording in article | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3,007 proteins | Significantly changed in measured abundance immediately after one cycling session across tested fluids | 3,007 new hormones or proven inter-organ messages |
| Organs talking | Proteomic patterns and known physiology support research into multi-organ communication | This study proved direct signals from muscle to brain or immune cells in every person |
| Discovery cohort | 25 healthy young men, one 45-minute bout | Large representative clinical trial of all exercise types |
| Validation | Many plasma responses replicated; selected candidate genes explored in flies | Clinical validation of a new human therapy |
| Glucose uptake | GLUT4 responds to both insulin and contraction via partly distinct signaling | Exercise replaces insulin or always lowers glucose |
| Exercise snacks | Four vigorous one-minute bouts a day over two days; small short-term glucose-pattern improvements | Four minutes of walking cures diabetes or replaces physical activity guidelines |
| Mean glucose | Between-condition change did not meet significance (p=0.07) | Average glucose significantly decreased |
| Disease endpoints | Molecular map generates hypotheses about cardiometabolic processes | Prevented diabetes, dementia, heart attack, or death |
| Sedentary breaks | Supported by ADA 2026 guideline as useful strategy | Guaranteed improvement for all people every 30 minutes |
| Memovela CTA | Helps users log activity and track habit history | Measures exerkines or provides clinical diagnosis |

### Pre-publication checks

- Confirm publisher title, publication date, DOI and main numerical claims with source PDFs or web HTML.
- Do not publish before editor reviews the science and safety note.
- Ensure all study links are clickable and cited at relevant claims.
- Confirm images include non-decorative alt text and captions, with conceptual disclaimer where needed.
- Site's publish date should equal actual deployment/publication date, never set automatically to the paper publication date.
- BlogPosting JSON-LD should use the actual article datePublished, not the study's 2026-10-12 publication date.
- Consider reviewing the displayed reading time against website's existing calculator.
