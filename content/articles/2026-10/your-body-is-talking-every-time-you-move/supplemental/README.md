# Mindful Diabetes — Supplemental Figures SEO Package

This package contains **five new branded educational figures** for the article:

**Your Body Is Talking Every Time You Move. Here's What Science Is Discovering.**

These images are designed to be added to the existing article package and article implementation. They extend the article with visuals that fill genuine teaching gaps and improve clarity.

## Included

- 5 PNG source images
- 5 optimized WebP versions for website use
- Figure-by-figure SEO metadata
- Captions
- Alt text
- Descriptions
- Suggested insertion points in the article
- Codex-ready integration instructions
- JSON and CSV image manifest files

## Recommended figure order in the article

1. **Figure 4** — Two 2026 studies, two different questions
2. **Figure 5** — What changes right away vs. what may adapt over time
3. **Figure 6** — Why glucose can move in different directions during exercise
4. **Figure 7** — A day with movement breaks
5. **Figure 8** — Choose movement that fits your body and day

## Primary integration rule

These are **supplemental figures** for the existing movement article. They should be integrated into the article body at the specified locations, using the provided captions and alt text, while preserving the rest of the article and earlier figures.

## Best practice

- Prefer the **WebP** versions on the website.
- Keep the **PNG** versions as archival or fallback assets.
- Preserve alt text and captions in HTML.
- Do not replace the existing hero image or earlier figures unless you explicitly decide to do so.
