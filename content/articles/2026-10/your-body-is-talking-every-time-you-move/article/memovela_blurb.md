## Extended in-app preview (~100 words)

What happens inside your body when you get up, walk, or ride a bicycle? Scientists measured more than 10,000 proteins across blood plasma, saliva, and urine following a 45-minute cycling session, finding thousands of significant changes immediately afterward. The study opens a window into the molecular responses that connect movement with metabolism, immunity, and recovery. We also look at why contracting muscles take up glucose and what a separate 2026 trial found about very short bouts of exercise. The takeaway is not to chase a perfect number. It is to build safe, realistic movement habits that fit your life.
